# Botox claim-integrity pass — 2026-08-03

## Outcome

The general Botox and Dysport service journey now stays inside the approved product and Rella fact boundaries while preserving its pricing, local intent, and two-clinic booking flow.

## Claims corrected

The prior acquisition copy said treatment could `prevent new wrinkles`, promoted preventive treatment by age bracket, suggested regular treatment could extend duration because `muscles adapt`, and characterized Dysport as spreading more and therefore being ideal for larger areas.

Those statements were removed because they were not established by the binding Rella fact set and were broader than the current official product language. The replacement copy now states:

- temporary improvement/softening of the appearance of dynamic lines;
- consultation-led, individualized treatment planning;
- Botox and Dysport are distinct prescription products;
- product units and dosing are not interchangeable;
- Botox softening may begin in 4–7 days and Dysport in 2–5 days;
- full effect is assessed around two weeks;
- results typically last 3–4 months and individual response varies;
- treatment generally has minimal downtime, with most patients able to return to their daily routine immediately;
- the repeat-treatment interval should be discussed with the provider rather than described as a touch-up schedule.

## Evidence boundary

- Binding Rella Napa tox facts supply the product-specific onset, two-week assessment, typical 3–4 month duration, and current pricing.
- Current FDA labeling describes BOTOX Cosmetic as providing temporary improvement in the appearance of indicated lines.
- Official BOTOX Cosmetic patient guidance supports minimal downtime and return to a daily routine immediately after leaving the specialist's office.
- No new efficacy percentage, ranking, prevention, safety guarantee, or product-superiority claim was added.

## Regression coverage

The service-catalog integrity suite now blocks the removed prevention, muscle-adaptation, spread, and touch-up-schedule phrases. It also requires the temporary-improvement framing, minimal-downtime boundary, and non-interchangeable-unit disclosure.

## Remaining clinical gate

The complete service catalog still requires final clinical review before production cutover. This pass reduces a known claims risk; it does not replace medical, regulatory, or legal review of the finished site.
