Confirm that the release should continue to avoid:

- guaranteed outcomes, response, or candidacy;
- universal `zero downtime`, `no downtime`, or immediate-results language;
- fixed doses, product amounts, procedure counts, maintenance schedules, or recovery windows unless supported by an approved service-specific source;
- disease-treatment, immunity, hangover-cure, migraine/pain-relief, prevention, or permanent-result claims in acquisition copy;
