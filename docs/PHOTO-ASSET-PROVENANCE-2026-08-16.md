## Release guard

- The user supplied these photos specifically through the Website Revamp folder and directed that they be added to the revamp.
- Identifiable patients and staff are used on the protected review preview. Written public-marketing consent remains a production-release check; the website must not imply that a visible treatment or device is more specific than the photo proves.
- `rella-team-storefront.webp` was verified as Napa by matching its ornate white doorway and pink multi-pane window treatment to the 1541 3rd St historic-house facade and the supplied Napa staff-photo backgrounds. It must not be labeled Vacaville or imply that every person pictured works only in Napa.
- No source image from the unrelated Drive mockup folder (for example `rella_exterior.png`) is treated as a real clinic exterior.
- The Messages originals were auto-oriented, cropped to 4:3, converted to sRGB WebP, and written without source EXIF, XMP, camera serial, or GPS metadata. The IV crop omits the visible lot/expiry area. The medical-weight-loss alt text never claims that the photographed syringes contain GLP-1 medication, semaglutide, or tirzepatide.
