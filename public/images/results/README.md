# Rella result-image intake

Place only publication-approved before-and-after images in this folder.

- General aesthetics/skin results: `main/`
- Medical weight-loss results: `weight-loss/`
- Use anonymous filenames such as `weight-loss-01-before.jpg` and `weight-loss-01-after.jpg`.
- Never use a patient name, date of birth, appointment date, chart number, or consent document in a filename or image.
- Keep before and after framing, crop, lighting, orientation, and color treatment as consistent as possible.
- Do not reshape, retouch, or otherwise alter the treatment area in a misleading way.
- Record the treatment and honest timeframe in `src/content/results.ts`.
- Start every entry as `draft`. Change it to `approved` only after Rella verifies written public-use permission in its private records.

Draft entries and unlisted files never render on the public pages.
